Build a mobile app primarily for iOS designed specifically for Gas Engineers in the UK as a replacement for “Gas Engineer Software”.

This app must not just be a basic invoicing app. It must be built around the real workflow of a gas engineer.

Main purpose

Help gas engineers manage:

customer details
appointments/jobs
gas safety records and certificates
servicing records
landlord/homeowner paperwork
quotes and invoices
sending documents by email or WhatsApp
Core gas engineer features

The app should include forms and workflows for:

CP12 / Landlord Gas Safety Record
Boiler service records
Breakdown / repair jobs
Installation records
Warning notices
Job notes and engineer observations

Each record should let the engineer enter things like:

customer name
property address
appliance type
appliance make and model
serial number
location of appliance
flue checks
ventilation checks
gas tightness / pressure test results
safety checks
defects found
remedial work carried out
engineer notes
date
engineer details
Gas Safe registration number
signature fields for engineer and customer
Documents

The app must:

generate professional PDF certificates and records
generate HTML versions of records where useful
allow previewing documents before sending
send documents by email
send documents by WhatsApp
save all previous records per customer and per property
Customer and property management
store multiple properties for one customer
save customer contact details
keep full service history for each property
allow quick repeat visits using previous appliance/property data
search customers, properties, and previous certificates easily
Job workflow

The app should support a real engineer workflow:

Create or open customer
Select property
Choose job type (CP12, boiler service, repair, installation, warning notice, quote, invoice)
Fill in engineer job form
Generate document/certificate
Get signatures
Send to customer by email or WhatsApp
Save to history
Invoicing features

Also include invoicing, but as part of the gas engineer workflow:

create quotes and invoices linked to a job
labour and materials breakdown
VAT support
mark paid/unpaid
generate PDF invoice
auto invoice numbers
UI and design
modern, professional, fast mobile interface
designed for engineers using it on-site
easy one-handed use
large buttons and simple forms
dark mode support
minimal taps to complete a certificate
Technical requirements
iOS-first mobile app
scalable codebase
secure cloud sync
offline support for working in properties with no signal
sync when internet returns
secure storage of customer and certificate data
Extra requirements
include sample gas engineer test data
create realistic screen designs for:
dashboard
customer list
property page
job form
certificate preview
invoice page
make the app feel like a real commercial field-service app for UK Gas Engineers
Important

Focus heavily on the gas engineer paperwork, certificates, service records, and job workflow.
This is not just a generic invoice app with a gas theme.
